# Incumbent map: where not to fight

Checked 2026-10-08. One paragraph per lane; identifiers live in `data/incumbents.json`. Dollar figures are USAspending prime awards, deduplicated across each company's UEIs, unless a linked source says otherwise. "Portfolio" names the Anti Fund companies in the lane; a new startup can be checked against each paragraph for whether it goes head-on, supplies the incumbents, or is truly new.

**Autonomous maritime** (portfolio: Saronic, Anduril). Saildrone is the Coast Guard's maritime-domain-awareness vendor: 60 awards, $72.5M, the latest a [$15.6M call order (Mar 2026)](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_70Z02326F75100002_7008_70Z02325A75100001_7008). BlackSea mass-produces the Navy's GARC drone boats in Baltimore and was [bought by AEVEX for up to $650M](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/08/aevex-to-acquire-blacksea-technologies-for-up-to-650m/); BlackSea has no prime awards of its own, but AEVEX has $1.66B (mostly ISR). HavocAI, now "Havoc", has [raised about $200M](https://www.workboat.com/autonomous-systems-startup-havoc-raises-100-million) but holds only $4M of Army SBIRs. Small attritable surface boats are now a volume fight between Saronic, BlackSea/AEVEX and Havoc.

**Small drones and loitering munitions** (portfolio: Anduril, Aeon, Kela). AeroVironment is the giant, with $7.3B across its own entities and BlueHalo, including a [$406.5M Switchblade production contract](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_W31P4Q20C0024_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-). Shield AI has [407 FAA-registered aircraft](https://registry.faa.gov/AircraftInquiry/Search/NameResult?Nametxt=SHIELD+AI&sort_option=1&PageNo=1) (405 V-BATs, 2 X-BATs). Red Cat's Teal Black Widow [took the Army SRR program of record from Skydio](https://www.therobotreport.com/red-cat-wins-u-s-army-next-gen-drone-contract-over-skydio/). Neros holds the Army's PBAS attritable-drone contract ([$17.5M first order, July 2026](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_W91CRB26FA251_9700_W91CRB26DA011_9700)). Skydio has 176 smaller federal buys ([e.g.](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_19GE5022P0087_1900_-NONE-_-NONE-)). The Army's two small-drone programs of record are taken; parts, radios and autonomy that feed all five are the open niches.

**Counter-drone and air defense** (portfolio: Anduril, Kela). SRC has $5.5B in awards across its UEIs, with 26 C-UAS award descriptions and the [DUKE V2 counter-IED jammers](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_0001_9700_W15P7T09DM615_9700). AeroVironment's BlueHalo sells the Titan system ([$29.0M Marine Corps order, 2025](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_M6785425C0104_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-)). Epirus's Leonidas microwave is the Army's [IFPC-HPM system](https://www.epirusinc.com/press-releases/epirus-receives-43-million-contract-from-u-s-army-for-ifpc-hpm-generation-ii-systems), though those deals do not show among its USAspending prime awards ($4.6M). DroneShield's US arm won a [$13.8M Army fixed-site award (June 2026)](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_W912CH26CA023_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-). Kela, mapped here and to drones, is an integration layer ([Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/article/partnering-with-kela-modern-defense-for-israel-and-western-allies)) rather than a sensor or effector maker.

**Autonomous ground vehicles** (no portfolio company). Applied Intuition says it is [valued at $15B](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/applied) and sells autonomy to defense. Forterra is the [renamed Robotic Research](https://www.trucknews.com/technology/robotic-research-rebrands-as-forterra/1003182227), still contracting under that name ($68.6M); its autonomy rides on Oshkosh vehicles in [ROGUE-Fires](https://www.forterra.com/posts/forterra-and-prime-contractor-oshkosh-defense-secure-u-s-marine-corps-rogue-fires-block-2-production-award). Overland AI has 9 Army, Navy and Marine Corps awards ([latest](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_W5170126CA063_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-)). Federal money here is small and flows through vehicle primes, so a newcomer would mostly be selling kits into Oshkosh-type platforms.

**Low-cost munitions and missiles** (portfolio: Anduril, Aeon). Castelion has $261M since 2023, led by a [$155M Navy order (Feb 2026)](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_N6833526F1022_9700_N6833526G0006_9700). X-Bow has $182M, mostly a [$129.5M motor-manufacturing contract](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_FA930025C6015_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-). Ursa Major has $43.5M, including a [$20M hypersonic engine award](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_FA930025C6011_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-), and Zone 5 has $29.4M ([Rusty Dagger](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_FA238526CB019_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-)). Solid rocket motors attract the largest checks; Aeon's [ZEUS anti-tank missile research](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_W911NF25PA007_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-) sits at the small end of this lane.

**Defense manufacturing** (portfolio: Anduril's Arsenal-1, Saronic's Port Alpha). Hadrian builds automated machining factories and won a [$39.2M Army award (Mar 2026)](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_W911RQ26CA011_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-). Divergent is [valued at $2.3B](https://bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-09-15/defense-tech-manufacturing-startup-divergent-valued-at-2-3-billion) and prints missile airframes for the primes. Machina Labs forms sheet metal robotically for the Air Force ([award](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_FA868425CB003_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-)). Firestorm sold the Air Force its container-sized [xCell drone factory](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_FA862926CB001_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-) and has 34 Tempest drones on the FAA registry. Most of these are suppliers to munitions and drone makers rather than rivals.

**Drone and robot components** (portfolio: Westmag, Efference). MP Materials now has the Pentagon as [largest shareholder, an NdPr price floor and a 10X magnet offtake](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1801368/000119312525157310/d43796dex991.htm). Vulcan Elements lined up a [$620M Office of Strategic Capital loan](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2757859-us-loans-vulcan-reelement-700mn-to-make-magnets). Both are magnet suppliers to motor makers like Westmag. Unusual Machines (NYSE American: UMAC) [opened a drone-motor factory in Orlando](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1956955/000168316825004261/umac_ex9901.htm) and is Westmag's most direct head-on rival. ModalAI, a flight-computer maker with [Army SBIR work](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_W5170125CA267_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-), is the closest analog to Efference.

**Nuclear fuel and enrichment** (portfolio: General Matter). DOE has given out three $900M enrichment task orders: [Centrus HALEU (July 2026)](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_89243226FNE400212_8900_89243225DNE000022_8900), [Orano LEU (Apr 2026)](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_89243226FNE400214_8900_89243225DNE000031_8900) and [General Matter HALEU (Mar 2026)](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_89243226FNE400213_8900_89243225DNE000023_8900). Urenco USA runs the [licensed Eunice, NM plant](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2532/ML25321A296.pdf). Orano is licensing an Oak Ridge plant (NRC 70-7038). GLE is [licensing a laser enrichment plant in Paducah](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2626/ML26265A220.pdf), General Matter's own town. X-energy's TRISO-X fuel plant has an [NRC environmental impact statement](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2603/ML26033A130.pdf). General Matter sits inside the incumbent set here, not outside it.

**Advanced fission** (no portfolio company; these are General Matter's customers). This is the most crowded lane. TerraPower has a [construction permit for Kemmerer Unit 1](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2608/ML26084A116.pdf) and a [$1.70B DOE award under US SFR Owner](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/ASST_NON_DENE0009054_089). X-energy holds a [$921.7M DOE ARDP award](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/ASST_NON_DENE0009040_089). Kairos holds [Hermes](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2333/ML23338A258.pdf) and [Hermes 2](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2435/ML24352A106.pdf) construction permits. Oklo runs an [active Aurora licensing project](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2627/ML26272A352.pdf), and Radiant has a [Part 70 application under review](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2627/ML26271A226.pdf). Valar [raised $1B](https://pulse2.com/valar-atomics-raises-1-billion-series-b-led-by-sequoia-and-secures-200-million-credit-facility/) after reaching criticality, and the NRC [opened its pre-application project](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2627/ML26271A241.pdf). Valar's $200M credit line was led by Erebor, also an Anti Fund company.

**Fusion** (portfolio: Helion). CFS raised [$863M (about $3B total)](https://cfs.energy/news-and-media/commonwealth-fusion-systems-raises-863-million-series-b2-round-to-accelerate-the-commercialization-of-fusion-energy) for SPARC and its Virginia ARC plant. TAE agreed a [$6B all-stock merger with Trump Media](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/trump-media-merge-tae-technologies-145054621.html). Pacific Fusion broke ground on a [~$1B Albuquerque campus](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/groundbreaking-for-pacific-fusion-campus/). Federal awards are trivial here (CFS: $2.8M), so private capital is the moat; supplying magnets, pulsed power or tritium handling is the realistic seed angle.

**Robot foundation models and humanoids** (portfolio: Physical Intelligence, Efference, Enigma). Figure is [valued at $39B](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/figure-reaches-39b-valuation-latest-164944663.html). Skild AI, at [$14B+](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/private-equity/robotics-startup-skild-ai-valued-above-14-billion-in-new-round), is the most direct rival to Physical Intelligence's cross-robot brain. Apptronik has [$935M+ in Series A funding](https://apptronik.com/news-collection/apptronik-closes-over-935-million-series-a). Agility is [going public at $2.5B](https://thenextweb.com/news/agility-robotics-public-spac-deal) with Digits already in warehouses. Boston Dynamics has the deepest federal history ([DARPA LS3](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_HR001110C0025_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-), $153M total).

**AI inference chips** (portfolio: Etched). NVIDIA paid a [reported ~$20B to license Groq's technology](https://www.constellationr.com/insights/news/nvidias-groq-deal-acquisition-acquihire-or-creative-licensing-deal). Cerebras [went public on Nasdaq in May 2026](https://www.cerebras.ai/press-release/cerebras-systems-announces-closing-of-initial-public-offering). Tenstorrent raised [about $700M at ~$2.6B](https://www.thetechoutlook.com/current-affairs/business/jeff-bezos-joins-samsung-and-lg-in-backing-ai-chipmaker-tenstorrent-at-2-6-billion-valuation/), and MatX [$500M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/24/nvidia-challenger-ai-chip-startup-matx-raised-500m/) for its LLM chip, the closest peer to Etched. Federal records are near-empty except Cerebras sales to DOE and the Army ($7.4M).

**Space logistics and propulsion** (portfolio: General Galactic). Firefly has $560M in federal awards, led by [NASA lunar-lander task orders](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_80JSC025F7026_8000_80HQTR19D0009_8000). Rocket Lab ($101M, 556 open roles) and Stoke both hold [NSSL Phase 3 Lane 1 seats](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_FA881125FB007_9700_FA881125DB007_9700), and Stoke raised a [$510M Series D](https://washingtontechnology.com/companies/2025/10/stoke-space-closes-510m-series-d-financing-round/408718). Impulse Space, General Galactic's closest head-on rival in orbital transfer, has $63.8M of Space Force and NASA work ([SBIR Phase III](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_FA880925CB001_9700_-NONE-_-NONE-)). Ursa Major supplies engines.

**Space compute** (portfolio: Orbital; SpaceX is also a portfolio company, so it is not listed). Starcloud flew an H100 to orbit and is [valued at $2.3B](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2026/08/21/orbital-data-center-startup-starcloud-valued-at-us23-billion-in-latest-funding/). Aetherflux renamed itself Cowboy Space and [raised $275M at $2B](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260508036993/en/Cowboy-Space-Corporation-Raises-%24275M-Series-B-For-Vertically-Integrated-Orbital-Data-Centers-and-Rockets) for upper-stage data centers. Axiom Space, NASA's $695M station and spacesuit contractor, [bought the first compute payloads on Kepler's relay network](https://kepler.space/kepler-announces-on-orbit-compute-capacity-on-optical-data-relay-network/). None has federal compute awards yet, so the lane is still forming.

**Tunneling and underground infrastructure** (portfolio: The Boring Company). Herrenknecht built the [$76M TBM for VTA's BART Silicon Valley extension](https://progressiverailroading.com/passenger_rail/news/VTA-OKs-tunnelboring-machine-for-BART-extension-project--70585), bought through the Kiewit Shea Traylor joint venture. Robbins is the [US hard-rock TBM maker since 1952](https://www.robbinstbm.com/about/). Federal records are thin: Traylor's only prime awards are two 2004-05 USACE contracts, because tunnel money flows through transit agencies' grants rather than to contractors' UEIs. Watch agency procurement, not USAspending, here.

**Brain-computer interfaces** (portfolio: Merge). Neuralink raised [$650M at about $9B](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-02/neuralink-raises-650-million-in-late-stage-funding-round). Synchron raised a [$200M Series D](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20251106150841/en/Synchron-Raises-%24200-Million-Series-D-to-Advance-Brain-Computer-Interface-Technology) for its pivotal trial. Precision has [FDA 510(k) clearance](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/fda-clears-precision-neurosciences-minimally-invasive-brain-computer-interface-implant). Blackrock's operating company has 154 [NIH and VA awards](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/ASST_NON_R44DC018757_075). Beware "SYNCHRON, LLC" in USAspending: it is a Navy contractor, not the BCI company.
